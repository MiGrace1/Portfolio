## Context

Solve'Educ is a platform for Cameroonian secondary education, built during my internship at WELLDONE&CO.

## Problem

Students have to choose their academic path, and both their grades and their conduct are useful inputs for that choice. The platform brings this information together to support the decision.

## My contribution

- Design and development of the platform, including UML modeling
- Backend built with Django and MySQL
- AI module that analyzes grades and conduct to suggest an academic path
- Personalized questionnaire for final-year students
