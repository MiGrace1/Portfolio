## Context

Solve'Shop is a marketplace for French-speaking Africa, where mobile money is a common way to pay.

## Problem

A marketplace for this region has to support mobile money payments, not just bank cards.

## My contribution

- Full design and development of the platform with Django
- Mobile money payment integration

## Technical challenges

The main challenge was mobile money payment: confirmation does not depend on the site, because customers approve the transaction on their phone. Pending, successful and failed payments all had to be handled, with orders kept consistent with their real state. The data model also had to organize the shops, products and orders of a multi-vendor marketplace.

## Outcome

The platform is complete but has not been deployed online.
